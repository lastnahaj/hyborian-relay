# Simple Minimap

**Source mod:** Simple Minimap (by Xevyr) v5.1.9 (`3719513784`)

Simple Minimap adds a movable minimap with adjustable scale, multiple zoom levels, points of
interest, and custom map markers.

## Player controls and features

- Hold **F1** for mouse interaction with the minimap interface.
- Choose from five zoom levels.
- Move the minimap by dragging the player icon.
- Create custom markers through the normal map.
- Share individual markers with online clan members.
- Use optional clan-member tracking where available.
