# Mystical Travel Shelf

**Source mod:** Mystical Travel Shelf (by Xevyr) (`3718830483`)

The Mystical Travel Shelf is a compact alternative to the standard maproom. It uses an aiming-based
destination system because its destinations cannot be selected by walking around a full-size
maproom.

Its recipe is associated with the Cartographer feat and is not positioned as a free substitute for
normal maproom progression.
