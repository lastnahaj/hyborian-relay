# Combat & Equipment

The Enhanced stack expands combat and equipment through several major systems. This section keeps
their roles separate and publishes item-level details only when normal player acquisition is
verified.

## Major systems

- [Highmane's Arsenal](./highmanes-arsenal.md) — a large weapons, armor, accessories, consumables,
  crafting, building, and companion catalog.
- [Armory and Arsenal](../progression/armory-and-arsenal.md) — classes, skill trees, equipment
  growth, and endgame progression.
- [Wings Of Valhalla](../progression/wings-of-valhalla.md) — weapon arsenals, equipment, and
  randomized crafting properties within a larger overhaul.
- [ExilesExtreme](./exiles-extreme.md) — player-accessible pets, mounts, equipment, cosmetics,
  structures, and placeables.
- [Domain's Magic](../magic/domain-magic.md) — interruptible casting and magical combat systems.

## PvP information standard

Future item entries will identify how an item is obtained, its weapon or equipment type, verified
player effects, and any confirmed server restriction. The public catalog does not include internal
identifiers or unverified acquisition routes.

For live combat feedback, see [The Damage Meter](../qol/damage-meter.md).
