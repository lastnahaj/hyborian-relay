## Deliberate exclusions

- Legacy Workshop pages and screenshots are not evidence for Enhanced behavior.
- General Workshop collections, third-party guides, comments, and community posts are not used as
  factual sources.
- Server-management manuals are not surfaced as player guidance. Only confirmed player-visible
  features belong in this wiki.
- No source image has been copied locally. The site remains text-only until an exact approved image
  can be stored with complete provenance.

The machine-readable audit, including source role and edition scope, is maintained in
`data/sources.json` and checked during every production build.
