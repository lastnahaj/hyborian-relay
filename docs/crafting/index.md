# Crafting & Resources

Several systems in the stack can affect crafting and resource handling:

- [Highmane's Arsenal](../combat/highmanes/crafting-building.md) documents universal benches and
  crafting expansions at the mod level; individual recipes require server verification.
- [Wings Of Valhalla](../progression/wings-of-valhalla.md) documents new tools, resources, storage,
  and randomized crafting properties.
- [Armory and Arsenal](../progression/armory-and-arsenal.md) documents craftable gear, gems,
  enchanting, and enhanced tools.
- [Unlock Plus](../building/unlock-plus.md) provides player-facing storage and crafting workflows.

No server-specific recipe, resource location, station requirement, or crafting value is published
until it is sourced and observed here.
