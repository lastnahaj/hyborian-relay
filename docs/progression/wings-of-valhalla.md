# Wings Of Valhalla

**Source mods:** Wings Of Valhalla (Beta) (`3719965485`) and Wings Of Valhalla Equipment (Beta)
(`3721036709`)

Wings Of Valhalla is one of the stack's largest progression and world-content systems. Its public
documentation describes the following player-facing families of features.

## Progression and activities

- Custom character progression and milestone rewards
- Five profession categories tied to core stats
- Bounties involving base-game and Wings Of Valhalla targets
- Extended follower leveling
- A central interface for Wings Of Valhalla systems

## Equipment and crafting

- Fifteen weapon arsenals
- Custom weapons and armor supplied with the companion equipment mod
- Crafting and equipment systems with randomized properties in the documented live build
- New tools, resources, storage, and sorting features

## Creatures and world areas

- Breedable Rocknoses and Dragons
- Documented custom areas including a player market, portal room, and resource mines

## Server verification boundary

Presence in the mod's documentation does not prove that a feature is enabled, available, or
unchanged on this server. Focused profession, bounty, equipment, breeding, and travel guides will be
published after each feature is observed here. The server's final progression table remains withheld
until its interaction with EAA is verified.
